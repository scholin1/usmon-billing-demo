We are leaning toward one layout. Does it hold up for you?

We compared several ways to work a claim, and we are leaning toward the one below, Familiar. It is closest to how you work today, it puts a whole claim on one page, and it lets you show and hide the columns you care about with no hopping between tabs. Before we commit, we want you to pressure-test it, especially when a claim is a mess.

The real test: on your busiest day, with a messy claim, does Familiar still work, or would you rather switch to another view? If so, which, and when?
