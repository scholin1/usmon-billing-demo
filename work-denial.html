Why was this claim denied?

CO-50 — Medical Necessity Not Established
N115 · MA01
Cigna is denying payment for intraoperative neurophysiological monitoring (IONM) on 07-14-2026 on the grounds that medical necessity was not demonstrated on the claim. This is a documentation denial, not a coverage exclusion — Cigna covers 95940/95941 when supported. N115 means the decision was based on clinical guidelines; MA01 means the claim was previously processed and this is a revised determination.
Most likely cause
CO-50 on IONM is almost always missing or insufficient clinical documentation. Cigna's guidelines require the operative report to include the surgeon's attestation that IONM was medically necessary for this specific procedure and patient. If the operative report does not include that language — or if it was not submitted — Cigna denies on medical necessity. Check that the attending surgeon signed an IONM attestation and that it was included with the original claim submission.
